themes applied

One screen, three people. The quiz runs once, the type is set, and from then on the surface belongs to them — while every control stays forest, so the product never has to re-learn what a button looks like.

Themed surfaces carry a single ink. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from lowering the opacity of the text — a 70% ink on a mid-tone surface is how a 7:1 ratio quietly becomes a 3:1 one.

what carries over

Surface and raised layer — the only two colours the theme owns. Cards are the raised step, never white.

Ink — one per theme, used at every size. The Planner's is borrowed from the core forest ramp because its own failed.

Nothing else. Buttons, type scale, radii, spacing and motion are identical across all three. Swapping a theme is three token values, not a redesign.

The emblems are placeholders standing in for the illustrated plant each type receives — a wildflower for the Seeker, per the wireframe.
