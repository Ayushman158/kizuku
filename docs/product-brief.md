# Product Brief

## Product

Kizuku is a wellness app for future anxiety: the loop of mentally simulating what could go wrong before anything has happened.

## Core Promise

Turn one future-facing worry into one grounded action every day.

## MVP User

High-achieving young adults who are functional on the outside but spend significant mental energy rehearsing possible futures. The product should feel warm and active, not clinical, gamified, or productivity-coded.

## Daily Loop

1. Ritual pause.
2. Write one worry.
3. Generate one action.
4. Do the action.
5. Reflect on what happened.
6. Grow the tree.

The loop resets daily. The app should avoid replay-heavy, metric-heavy, and streak-heavy mechanics.

## Differentiators

- Future anxiety as a specific product wedge.
- Action generation instead of passive calming.
- Tree growth as emotional labor, not time served.
- No streaks or punishment for missing days.
- Lowercase, quiet, non-institutional copy.

## MVP Non-Goals

- Diagnosis or therapy claims.
- Social feed.
- Public progress sharing.
- Streaks, leaderboards, achievements, or daily guilt mechanics.
- Complex journaling archive in the first release.
